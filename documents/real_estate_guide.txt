Real Estate Buying Guide & Investment Tips:
1. Legal Verification: Always verify sub-registrar records and search title documents for 30 years clear title before purchasing property in Pakistan.
2. Property Taxes: Stamp duty is approximately 2%, Capital Value Tax (CVT) is 1%, and registration fees vary by province.
3. Market Valuation: High-density areas generally show a 10-12% annual appreciation rate compared to emerging suburbs.
4. Construction Costs: Standard residential construction costs range between PKR 3,500 to PKR 5,000 per sqft depending on finish quality.
5. Inspection Checklist: Inspect foundation dampness, plumbing pressure, electrical load approvals, and setback area compliance.
